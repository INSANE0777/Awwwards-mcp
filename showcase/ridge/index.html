About Ridge

Ridge was founded on a simple observation: the best quantitative researchers and infrastructure engineers don't apply to jobs. They get one shot every few years to make a move, and they want that conversation to be with someone who actually understands the work — the systems, the trade-offs, the trade-offs behind the trade-offs. We spent twelve years inside those rooms. Now we run the search the way we wish someone had run it for us.
