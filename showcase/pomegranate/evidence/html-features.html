Your health isn't a collection of isolated appointments, test results and insurance claims. It is a constantly changing story. Pomegranate brings together information about your health, activity, behaviour, environment, and medical history to build a continuously evolving picture of you—not simply what's already happened, but where you may be heading.

Pomegranate cares about you all the time

Learning
about
you

Most healthcare starts again with every appointment, provider or annual insurance policy. Pomegranate is different. It is designed to remember, learn and improve its understanding of you over time—helping it recognise meaningful change and give you increasingly relevant insight. The longer you use Pomegranate, the more it learns — and the more useful it becomes.

Population averages only tell you so much. Pomegranate shows you how you compare with people who meaningfully resemble you — across the things that matter to your health.
See where you're doing better. Where you're doing worse. What's changing. And, eventually, what happened next to people whose health looked rather like yours.

The things that affect your health change wherever you live or travel — climate, air quality, food, sleep, activity and more. Pomegranate is designed around you, not healthcare systems —building a continuous health history that moves with you, wherever your life may take you.

Subscription-based healthcare

Healthcare

Everyone gets sick. Insurance is designed for the unpredictable. Pomegranate is designed for the everyday.
A continuing health subscription can focus on prediction, prevention, early intervention and care. Insurance can still protect against rare, catastrophic costs — without defining the rest of your healthcare.

Your health information is some of the most personal data you have. You control what you connect, what you share and how your information is used. Pomegranate is being engineered so intelligence doesn't have to come at the expense of privacy. Privacy, security and consent are designed and engineered into the fabric of Pomegranate.
