23# What is Webflow, and why host 
24my website there?
25
26‍Webflow is a cloud-based platform 
27that combines a website builder, 
28design tool, and CMS. It lets you 
29create responsive websites with a 
30drag-and-drop interface, 
31streamlining the process compared 
32to traditional methods.
33
34It also empowers your marketing 
35team to manage and update the 
36website easily.
37
38
39‍---
40
41
42# Do you work with platforms 
43other than Webflow?
44
45No, we specialize exclusively 
46in Webflow. It allows us to 
47deliver faster, cleaner, 
48and more reliable websites.

54‍# Can you work with clients 
55outside of your timezone?
56
57Yes. We are based in Poland and 
58work in the CET/CEST timezone, 
59which aligns well with clients 
60in Europe, the Middle East, 
61and the US East Coast.
62
63We can also collaborate with clients 
64on the US West Coast or in the APAC 
65region, but these projects are 
66usually async-first. If you need 
67frequent live calls, we will be 
68upfront about whether the schedule 
69is a good fit.

75# What do you need from us before 
76we can start?
77
78The essentials: logo files and 
79brand assets, any existing brand 
80guidelines, content for each page, 
81or confirmation that copywriting 
82is included in the project scope, 
83access to your domain and hosting, 
84examples of websites you like 
85and dislike.

88useful than the inspiration board.

90paragraphs in a Google Doc, is a 
91perfectly good starting point.
