Feature

Field notes from the dormant months — what soil remembers when nothing is planted.

From the editors

We believe that rest is not absence. Between plantings, a field is neither empty nor idle: roots hold, microbes negotiate, and the ground quietly writes the next season. Fallow Press documents these intervals — ecological, cultural, and personal — one issue at a time.
