Practices

Four chapters on how the press works — the paste, the ink, the rest, and the people. Each one held still while the ground passes.

01

Chapter one

The paste

Every issue starts on a table in Reviewed. Clippings, seed catalogs, postcards from readers — the paste-up is physical before it is digital, and the grain of the board shows in the layout.

02

Chapter two

The ink

We print in two passes: a warm black for the photographs, a mono pink — the press pink, N°FF9398 — for the things we cannot photograph. The second pass is the point.

“We rest the press between volumes. The rest is part of the work.”

The masthead, Issue N°03

03

Chapter three

The rest

Between volumes the site lies quiet — no new stories, small dispatches only. Fields taught us this: attention, like soil, needs a season of nothing to stay good.

04

Chapter four

The people

Fallow Press is five people and a board of readers. No ads, no grants that bend the masthead — the library and the print run carry the work.
